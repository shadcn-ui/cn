Axis utilities now override the logical sides they cover, matching tailwind-merge 3.7.0. `px-2` replaces `ps-*` and `pe-*`, `py-2` replaces `pbs-*` and `pbe-*`, and the same applies to `mx`/`my`, `inset-x`/`inset-y`, `border-x`/`border-y` widths and colors, and `scroll-mx`/`scroll-my`/`scroll-px`/`scroll-py`.
